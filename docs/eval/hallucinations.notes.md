**Status: interim. The structure below is measured; what each case IS (invention, a wrong label, or a definition question) needs a person looking at the pages, and that verdict is not in yet.** The list for that is `outputs/hallucination_review.md` (not committed: it holds evidence quotes). Nothing in `ground_truth/` or `gt_from` was changed.

**1. One label family produces almost all the scalar cases.** 11 of the 12 scalar hallucinations sit on four documents that share doc_016's blind label: doc_016 itself (1) and the three phone photos doc_032, doc_033, doc_034 (3, 4 and 3). The label describes an electricity-token purchase (one row, total 503100, no vendor, no date). The audit flags all three photos as suspect (1 of 5 labeled non-null fields right, against 4 of 5 for the owner), and the run reads them as three different receipts: four distinct totals over the four documents (`503100`, `36000`, `357091`, one abstained), four distinct dates (one of them "absent"), and document numbers that differ. Copies of one page do not read like that. Two explanations fit and the tool cannot tell them apart: (A) the three photos are different documents that were given doc_016's label through `gt_from`, so they are scored against the wrong label; (B) they are photos of the same page and the model invented three complete, different receipts from blur. (A) is the more likely reading, because the model's reads are specific and internally consistent (item rows, a seller line, "toko" next to a shop name), but that is my inference from the model's output, not something I saw on the page.

**2. If (A) holds, the headline moves, and by a lot.** A sensitivity calculation, NOT a result: leaving doc_032, 033 and 034 out of `test-g1` gives 257 of 284 fields correct (0.905, against 0.845), and the scalar hallucination rate falls from 12 of 116 labeled-absent fields (10.3%) to 2 of 92 (2.2%). The two that remain are doc_016 `vendor_name` ("Tokopedia", where the label says no vendor; the model's own note says no explicit seller is named) and doc_014 `subtotal` (the model read the only printed amount, a "Harga Jual" figure, as the subtotal, confidence 0.6). If (B) holds, those 10 cases are real and serious: confidence 0.5 to 0.95, including a payment method (`QRIS`, 0.95) and a date (`2026-10-06`, 0.95).

**3. Whichever it is, the abstention score does not see them.** 9 of the 27 cases (3 scalar, 6 cell) have a confidence of 0.90 or more, and 13 more are between 0.70 and 0.89. Only 3 of the 27 carry a validator code (`components_ne_total`, `lines_ne_subtotal`, `document_date_in_future`) and all three were kept at 0.5 to 0.7. All 27 were already in the model's own answer: no validator invented any value, and none withheld one. The grounding check cannot catch these: the quote and the value were produced together (the limit `validate/grounding.py` states).

**4. The 15 row-cell cases are mostly a row-definition question, not invention.** doc_019 (8 cells over 4 rows) and doc_035 (2 cells): the model returned shipping fees, platform fees, vouchers and discount lines as line items, and says so in its own notes ("Shipping-fee row on the shipping receipt page", "Discount row counted as a line item"). The labels do not list those rows, so each lands as a matched pair whose description and amount cells the label leaves null. doc_014 (2 cells, quantity 3 and a unit price of 5000000): the model's note says an HS code was treated as a code, and the label has no quantity or unit price for that row. doc_029 (3 cells): a "Big Iced Tea" row with amount 0 and quantity 2. Whether a fee, voucher, discount or zero-amount item is a line item is a labeling policy (`ground_truth/LABELING.md`), and the comparison depends on it: the model's choice is defensible and so is the label's. These 15 rose from 12 in `test-final` only because the grounding fix (G1) restored a doc_029 row (3 cells) that used to be abstained, and so was counted as one missed plus one spurious row; it is not a new mistake.

**What I would do with the verdicts.** If the owner finds (A): relabel doc_032, 033 and 034 as their own documents (a prelabel by a model other than the extractor, then a human verification, exactly as the other non-blind documents; they cannot be called blind any more, because their model output has been seen), remove `gt_from` from them, record it in `ground_truth/CHANGELOG.md`, rescore, and quote the new numbers with this note beside them. If (B): keep the labels, and the 10 scalar cases become the first concrete evidence for the hallucination probe (S4): a model that invents whole receipts from a blurred photo at confidence 0.9.
