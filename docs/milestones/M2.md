Other things for you to decide:
- **Bundles:** `doc_017` has 78 pages and `doc_010` has 20. They need `page_range` rows before they are usable.
- **Repository size:** all 31 documents ingest to about 88 MB, and `doc_017` alone is 51 MB. Consider a lower DPI or cap before committing pages.
- **Manifest file names:** review them before you commit `data/manifest.csv`.
- **Half-committed constructed set:** the labels `ground_truth/cons_00{1,2,3}.json` are committed, but their manifest rows are not. A fresh checkout will therefore flag them as orphans until you commit the manifest.
- **Pre-labeling setup:** add the `PRELABEL_*` block from `.env.example` to your `.env`. I never touched `.env`.
- **Two defaults to check:** `assign_split` sets every non-blind hand-labeled document to `label_mode=prelabel`, and the blind count defaults to 10. With 25 documents, that is a large share of your test set.
- **Dependencies:** the default install groups now include Augraphy (via the `degrade` group) and Streamlit (via the `tools` group), so a fresh `uv sync` is heavier.
